Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the use of our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring that your health and fitness data remains strictly confidential and secure.

Core Privacy Commitment: The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user information. We do not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising frameworks. All data remains exclusively on your individual device.

1. Apple HealthKit Integration

Our App utilizes Apple's HealthKit framework to read and write your weight and related health information. We do this solely to provide you with weight tracking and fitness history features within the App interface.

  • User Consent: You must explicitly grant the App permission to access HealthKit. You can modify or revoke these permissions at any time through your iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Health.
  • No External Storage: Any health data pulled from HealthKit is processed in-memory or stored locally on your device. We do not transfer, upload, or sync this data to any external cloud infrastructure or third-party servers.
  • Strict Data Usage Restrictions: In accordance with Apple's Developer Program License Agreement, we do not sell your health data, nor do we use it for advertising, marketing, profiling, data mining, or any commercial purposes outside of providing the core functionality of the App.

2. Information Collection and Use

Because the App operates entirely locally on your iOS device:

  • We do not collect personal identifiers (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or device IDs).
  • We do not collect account credentials; no registration or log-in is required to use the App.
  • We do not collect usage diagnostics, crash logs, or location telemetry that could be linked back to you.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party advertising networks, data brokers, cloud storage backends, or analytics platforms. Your data is never shared with anyone.

4. Children's Privacy

Since the App does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it naturally does not collect any personal information from children under the age of 13, adhering strictly to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

6. Contact Us
